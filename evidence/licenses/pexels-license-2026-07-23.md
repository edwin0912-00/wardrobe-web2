# Pexels license evidence snapshot

- Source: https://www.pexels.com/license/
- Retrieved: 2026-07-23T09:40:00Z
- Publisher: Pexels, a Canva Germany GmbH brand
- Evidence role: license terms for the Pexels source-inspiration pages named by the Zeely scene catalog.

Verified license facts at retrieval:

- Pexels states that its photos and videos are free to use.
- Attribution is not required.
- Modification is allowed.
- Online, advertising and marketing use are listed as permitted examples.
- Unaltered resale, false endorsement, stock-service redistribution and trademark use are restricted.

Zeely use is narrower than the licensed allowance: the source pages are used only to extract role-limited observations. Source pixels are not shipped in a Zeely preset and have no authority over identity, body, hair, outfit, brands, readable text or exact architecture.
