# Source observation — `shoot.duckweed_forest_ophelia`

## Authority and reconciliation

This log was written after opening all eleven photographic source files at
native resolution. The source directory was
`/private/tmp/creative-universe-src.2QHBQA/alaska`. The files form one coherent
fashion shoot across a dry woodland edge and an adjacent duckweed-covered
water surface. The recurring white gathered blouse, burnt-orange lower garment,
wet duckweed debris, subject, low warm sun and dark forest planes link the two
chapters. No legacy generated sheet was used as source authority.

Source identity is expressly excluded. Facial proportions, eye colour,
freckles, piercings and other person-specific marks visible in these images are
not style and must not transfer. Camera body, lens model, focal length,
aperture, shutter, ISO/stock and exact site geometry are not recoverable from
the pictures and remain unknown.

## Shoot-wide invariants

- One dark woodland/duckweed world with a bright far plane and a load-bearing
  near-black tree mid-plane.
- Low warm sun appears as direct patches or a hair/back rim; cool open shade is
  the fill. Direct versus shaded face is shot-specific.
- Clean optics: no authored flare shape, glow, bloom, halation, vignette,
  chromatic fringe or swirl.
- Focus depth follows scale: close portraits are shallow; full-length and wide
  environmental frames retain more context.
- The body is quiet but sculptural, using off-axis necks, arched torsos,
  angular elbows and relaxed wrists.
- Reflections, compact occlusion and near limbs are distinct frame devices,
  never stacked into every shot.
- Dry cloth hangs in gathered or broad gravity folds; wet cloth clings,
  darkens, can turn partially translucent and carries duckweed specks.

## Palette observation

The closed environmental/styling set is forest green-black, duckweed green,
sunlit acid foliage, slate open water, cool open sky, burnt orange, off-white
cloth, warm reflected water and magenta makeup. Skin, hair, eyes and the
approved inserted item's exact colours remain exempt. Blue sky is visibly
source-backed in `frame-01.jpg`, `frame-04.jpg` and `frame-11.jpg`.
