## PER-FRAME READ

### frame-01.jpg

Vertical, centered institutional hall. A seated foreground subject occupies the lower middle while
rows of seated figures and identical chrome-legged desks recede behind. Circular luminous ceiling
wells dominate more than half the frame. The camera is close to seated chest/eye height, nearly
level, and held on the central axis; desk rows compress into symmetry. Foreground desk rails and
legs form repeated verticals. Focus is strongest on the front subject and falls gradually through
the rows. Light is diffuse, top-down, and green-olive; the floor falls near black. The front head
turns sideways with chin level; shoulders stay low, hands/arms rest, and there is no smile.

### frame-02.jpg

Horizontal, frontal and strongly centered. A dark hooded foreground figure sits at a pale table
with both hands planted wide and a clipboard centered between them. A black/red tabletop device
enters near the lower-left lens plane. Symmetric white-clad rows and circular ceiling wells recede
behind and are visibly softer. The foreground figure is nearly black against the olive-pale room;
the face is withheld by the hood. Camera roll is level. The table edge creates a hard lower frame
and the near device supplies deliberate foreground interference.

### frame-03.jpg

Horizontal exterior portrait from far below waist level, tilted upward. A dark, masked figure and
raised tubular accessory occupy the lower centre against a monumental pale arch and radial
glass/metal geometry. Strong convergence and near-object enlargement are visible; sky fills the
upper-left field. The dark material remains textured rather than crushed, while warm metal catches
small hard highlights. The exact mask, instrument, costume, and building are source designs and
must not transfer; only the low sculptural consequence, scale relationship, and material response
transfer.

### frame-04.jpg

Vertical profile portrait. The subject sits in the lower third against a wall of tall repeated
vertical ribs; pale cloudy sky opens at right. Camera is around upper-torso/shoulder height and
slightly below the face, with upright verticals and a compressed wall plane. Face and near shoulder
are sharp; ribs and distant roof blocks soften. Warm low sun cuts across the profile and pale
fabric, producing a clear terminator without flare. Pose chain: torso profile to frame right,
shoulders down, neck long, chin level, arms quiet below crop. Mouth is softly parted; gaze leaves
the lens.

### frame-05.jpg

Horizontal low frontal group portrait inside an unfinished concrete shell. A dark central figure
stands with hands clasped low; ranks of pale-clad people build a triangular formation around and
behind. Raw concrete slabs, columns and voids fill the frame; two distant dark figures stand on
upper ledges. Directional overhead/open-side light creates hard modelling on faces and cloth,
while the central dark figure remains a near-black anchor. The camera is level and low enough to
give the formation monumentality. No body is in motion; expressions are held, unsmiling, and
mostly directed past the lens.

### frame-06.jpg

Very wide horizontal interior shell. Repeated figures stand across floor and upper ledges; a long
diagonal concrete stair crosses the centre. The lower figures repeat an angular wrist-hand gesture
in profile/three-quarter views. Bright high openings send directional shafts through visible haze;
the lower floor and deep bays fall near black. Camera is distant and level. Architecture supplies
large depth planes, while people remain legible but secondary. Haze is atmospheric in this frame,
not evidence of a lens diffusion effect.

### frame-07.jpg

Extreme architectural wide. Stacked exposed slabs, beams and rust-streaked columns form large
rectangular modules. One person stands on a right-side upper tier under warm reflected ceiling
light; the figure is smaller than the product's minimum wide-shot coverage and must be enlarged to
the product lock if this composition is adapted. Camera is distant and slightly below the tier.
No foreground occluder is present. The source building is recognisable and therefore cannot be
copied; only generic modular concrete, warm apertures and human-to-structure scale transfer.

## RECONCILIATION ACROSS THE SEVEN FRAMES

This is one coherent institutional-modernist shoot, not two shoots. It deliberately alternates
between a green-olive interior hall, warm exterior ribs, and a raw shell with cool directional
haze. The constants are monumental repeated geometry, still human formations, low or central-axis
cameras, pale concrete/cloth against deep charcoal, and restrained saturation.

The fixed optical reading is clean and rectilinear: crisp architectural edges, no repeated flare,
no swirl, no chromatic fringe, no vignette, and no global diffusion. The haze in `frame-06.jpg` is
an environment layer. The sunlit exterior and green interior are per-shot lighting states inside
the same palette, not permission to invent a third state.

Reference people, faces, bodies, masks, garments, instruments, desks and controls are not identity
or product authority. Their transferable facts are posture, repetition, material behaviour,
occlusion, and tonal mass. Any generated architecture must be original and non-recognisable.

## TRANSFERABLE EXPRESSION AND GARMENT BEHAVIOUR

Expression stays ceremonial: no smile, cheekbones at rest, lids neutral or low, mouth closed or
softly parted, chin level, gaze directed sideways/past the lens or deliberately withheld. Provenance:
frames 01, 02, 04, 05.

Cloth is mostly still and opaque. Structured layers hold broad folds and their own volume; long
layers hang plumb; no wind-driven flutter is visible. Pale surfaces take hard or diffuse light
without glossy hotspots, while dark matte layers retain only low-detail folds. Apply those
behaviours to the approved item without copying source cut, colour, pockets, straps, mask, or
uniform design. Provenance: frames 01–05 and 07.
