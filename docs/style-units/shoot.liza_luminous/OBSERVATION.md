# OBSERVATION — `shoot.liza_luminous` (portfolio shoot `liza`)

Read 2026-07-27 from the originals downloaded off `edwinnovak.com/liza` via the Wix media CDN,
10 frames. Read as a normalised contact sheet at 300px per tile: enough for composition, palette,
light direction and garment state; **not** enough for skin texture, grain or eyelid detail, and
nothing of that kind is asserted below.

## What is unambiguous

**This is a fashion editorial with one model**, unlike `zayn`. So it can legitimately carry
person, expression and garment-behaviour observations — though never authority over a user's own
outfit.

**Camera.** Consistently low, tilted up, so the horizon sits at or below the subject's waist and
the sky becomes the backdrop. Several frames are close and wide-angle enough that the near
shoulder or hand distorts toward the lens. One frame is a top-down of the model lying in grass.

**Light.** Warm hard low sun, mostly behind or across the subject, so the sheer fabric is lit
through rather than on. Backlight blows the sky to white at the horizon in two frames and holds
deep saturated blue at the zenith in others. One frame is a backlit silhouette in mist with a
visible flare disc — the only optical artefact seen.

**Palette, counted from what is visible.** Deep sky blue; a white-to-pale-cream fabric; warm
skin; green grass; white flower heads with a green calyx. Five families, no sixth, no black.

**Environment.** Open field of tall grass, no architecture at all, no horizon furniture. Sky is
the dominant surface in most frames.

**Garment behaviour — the signature of the shoot.** Sheer translucent white textile, several
layers, wide enough to catch air. It streams sideways off the body in wind, holds a curved
airborne edge away from the shoulder, and transmits the sun so the limb behind it stays visible.
It never drapes heavily and never creases sharply. Where it lies on grass it spreads flat.

**Subject state.** Head tilted back or turned into the light, eyes narrowed or closed against the
sun, mouth closed and soft, arms raised or extended away from the body. Held poses, not motion
snapshots, except where wind moves the cloth.

## UNKNOWN — recorded rather than guessed

- Focal length; consequences recorded instead.
- Whether the blown highlights are exposure or grade.
- Skin, grain and eyelid detail: not readable at 300px tiles.
- Frames were not individually read at full width; only the contact sheet was.

## Why this shoot is the source for `editorial.edwin_novak.luminous_blue_white`

The config describes that mode as "blue sky, white translucent textile, grass or flowers and warm
hard sun with clear material detail". All five terms are present. The mode is blocked only because
it declares one source where the catalogue requires two, so this observation does not unblock it.
