# OBSERVATION LOG — shoot.overcast_street_stride
Source: frame-01, one supplied retail frame. Extraction-only. Garment design does not transfer.

## CAMERA POSITION
Chest-to-eye height, level, roll level. Long-lens character.
Read from: distant facades held large relative to the subject, street depth compressed but legible.

## PERSPECTIVE CHARACTER
Compressed: background pedestrians and cars read close behind the subject. Verticals do not converge.

## DEPTH OF FIELD
Subject sharp head to shoe. Mid-ground pedestrians slightly soft, far end softer. Gradual falloff.

## FOREGROUND OCCLUSION
None.

## OPTICAL SIGNATURE (fixed)
Clean, neutral, fine grain. No flare (no sun disc), no halation, no vignette.

## LIGHT
Bright overcast dome, sun high behind cloud. Shadows soft, short, directly underfoot.
Gentle contrast, neutral colour throughout — nothing warms, nothing blooms.

## HOW SKIN TAKES THIS LIGHT
Deep dark skin under an overcast dome: highlights are broad and soft — forehead, cheekbones,
nose bridge — fed by the whole sky, so they sit wide and low-contrast. No hard terminator anywhere:
the light wraps fully around the face. Matte overall with a soft sheen on the highest planes only.
Undertone stays neutral; no cast from the environment.

## GARMENT BEHAVIOUR (transfers)
Caught mid-stride, driven by the subject's own walk. An open outer layer swings — hem lifts
slightly, fronts part. Wide trouser legs ripple and trail at the leading leg. Suede-class surface:
matte, light-absorbing, zero specular. A soft bag crumples under the arm.
TRANSFER: garments in this unit move with the walk; an open layer swings, a wide leg ripples;
matte surfaces stay matte — nothing blooms or shines.

## EXPRESSION & GAZE
Body in profile stride; head turned toward the lens, gaze into it — that tension is the signature.
Lids neutral, brows at rest, zygomatic at rest, mouth closed, jaw relaxed.
