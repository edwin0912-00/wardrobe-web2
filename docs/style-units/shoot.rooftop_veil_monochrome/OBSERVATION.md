# Source observation — `shoot.rooftop_veil_monochrome`

## Authority and reconciliation

This log was written after opening all nine photographic source files at native
resolution from `/private/tmp/creative-universe-src.2QHBQA/naked`. They form one
coherent monochrome rooftop fashion shoot: the same parapet, mattress/bedding,
anonymous city, flat overcast dome, sheer layers, body-led posing and visible
full-frame grain recur throughout. No generated legacy board is source
authority.

The photographed person's face, haircut, ear and piercing configuration,
tattoos, skin marks and garment motifs are identity/styling particulars, not
transferable shoot grammar. A future approved user identity and approved item
must remain the only authority for those particulars.

## Shoot-wide invariants

- Monochrome value rendering with no delivered chroma.
- Uniform silver-halide-like grain remains visible across sky, skin,
  architecture and city.
- Blacks are slightly lifted and highlights roll softly; the overcast sky
  retains tone instead of clipping.
- No visible flare, halation, vignette or glow; mild corner softness is
  compatible with the set.
- The overcast sky is the broad source, creating soft transitions and little
  to no crisp cast shadow.
- Eyes are usually fully closed and unsqueezed; the single open-eye state looks
  past the lens.
- Body geometry alternates curled, folded, self-embracing, standing,
  over-coping and supine. Contact with architecture is shot-specific.
- Roll is common but not universal. Camera height ranges from mattress level to
  high oblique and steep downshot.
- Focus depth varies. Close and downshot frames can carry obvious optical
  background falloff; wide frames preserve more of the city.
- Sheer cloth may lie, crumple, veil the lens or lift into the air, while the
  light remains flat and non-glowing.

## Palette observation

Because the delivered photographs are monochrome, the closed palette is a
six-step value ladder: ink black, lace charcoal, city grey, parapet stone,
bedding white and overcast-sky light. True surface hues are destroyed by the
grade and remain unknown. Skin, hair, eyes and approved item colours are exempt
at input, but the final styled output remains monochrome.

## Legacy rejection

The previously generated `naked` pack was inspected and rejected as
authority. Its camera board falsely required deep focus/haze-only softness,
despite visible optical falloff in `frame-02.jpg` and `frame-06.jpg`. Its
blocking board falsely required prone architecture contact across the unit. Its
environment board invented north, metric scale, an exact roof plan, camera
stations, unseen reverse angles, sound and smell. Its person board encoded
source-person facial, piercing, mark and tattoo particulars. The historical
generation receipt remains rejected audit evidence only.
