## Coherence finding

`COHERENT_SOURCE_SET`. All sixteen frames belong to one transferable environmental and optical system:

- the same mid-autumn park vocabulary repeats: grass, leaf litter, dry weeds, yellow foliage, dark trunks and clear or white-bright sky;
- recurring people, dried flowers and carried textile forms connect the environmental, crop and detail registers;
- the daylight relationship is stable: a low hard warm source plus cool open-sky shade, seen from different camera positions;
- the clean baseline has smooth shallow bokeh, no visible swirl, no deliberate chromatic fringe and no halation ring;
- `frame-02.jpg` and `frame-08.jpg` form one deliberate interference register, not a different shoot, because the same palette, light warmth, people and carried materials remain visible through the plastic;
- all bodies and cloth are still; no frame introduces wind, running, jump timing or motion blur.

No frame was excluded from the supplied renumbered set. The legacy text that excluded old frames 05, 15 and 19 refers to a different numbering before interstitial removal and must not be applied to these current files.

## Transfer boundaries

The source people, faces, hair, garments, prints, jewellery, flowers-as-product styling and textile/bag construction are not identity or item authority. They may support camera consequence, joint chain, focus hierarchy, material response, carrying behavior and colour observations only. Generated work must use the approved user identity and approved items. The two anatomy sheets therefore require a generic white adult reference that does not resemble any source person.
