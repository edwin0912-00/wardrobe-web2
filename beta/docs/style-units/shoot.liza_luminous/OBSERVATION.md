## PER-FRAME READ

### frame-01.jpg

Vertical full-body environmental frame in tall grass. The subject stands/walks toward camera with
the low sun directly behind the head, producing a largely withheld face, a broad white-gold veil,
and one small pink flare disc near the torso. The sky occupies most of the frame; grass and seed
heads cross the lower body and foreground. A loose sleeve/outer layer lifts sideways. The camera
is around grass/waist height and tilted up slightly. Backlight transmits thin edges while opaque
layers remain dark. The flare disc is unique to this frame and is not a series-wide optical
constant.

### frame-02.jpg

Vertical close low-angle portrait against saturated deep blue sky. The nearest shoulder and torso
enlarge toward the lens while the head remains fully visible. Gaze points down toward the camera;
upper lids are neutral-low, brows at rest, mouth softly closed/parted, and cheekbones are not
smiling. Hard warm sunlight strikes the face and hair. A translucent textile streams off to frame
right and remains slightly softer than the face. No foreground object crosses the lens.

### frame-05.jpg

Vertical extreme low-angle sculptural three-quarter/body frame. One hip and lower torso enlarge in
the near plane; the torso rises away from camera; both shoulder-elbow-wrist chains extend outward.
Long translucent textile wraps across the torso, passes over the head, and streams laterally in
wind. Hard warm light makes overlapping layers visible against blue sky. Face and central torso
are sharp; extended fabric edges soften slightly. The exact source garment is not transferable.

### frame-06.jpg

Vertical extreme close view from below. Large white flower heads and green leaves fill the near
left, lower, and right edges and occlude part of the face, including one eye region. The visible
eye, nose and relaxed slightly parted mouth remain legible; the nearest petals and face share a
shallow focus band while farther flowers soften. Pale blue sky is the clean background. This is the
shoot's strongest observed foreground-interference geometry.

### frame-07.jpg

Horizontal top-down frame of a person lying in dense tall grass. The body forms a bent zigzag:
one knee rises, the opposite leg extends, one elbow bends over the head, and the other hand holds
flower stems across the torso. Grass surrounds and partly crosses the clothing and hair. Hard
warm sun creates crisp highlights and leaf/grass shadows. The subject fills most of the frame,
with flowers acting as a central material anchor. This is the only overhead viewpoint.

### frame-08.jpg

Vertical low close portrait against clear blue sky. Hair crosses the mouth and one eye region; a
raised translucent layer/limb-like cloth shape rises behind at frame left. The visible eye looks
down toward camera, lids low, brows at rest, mouth relaxed under hair. Hard sun creates bright skin
and cloth while the sky stays saturated. No flare or global bloom is visible.

### frame-09.jpg

Vertical low medium portrait against a nearly blown pale sky. The subject holds a bouquet near the
chest; broad sleeves and ruffled edges occupy the foreground/lower half and provide human context
around hands and stems. Head remains visible, gaze directed down/off camera, mouth relaxed. Light
is back/side and softer in the face than frames 02/05/08; translucent sleeve edges glow. No fixed
flare disc is present.

## RECONCILIATION ACROSS THE SEVEN FRAMES

This is one coherent luminous field shoot. Camera position varies intentionally between very low
sky portraits, one grass-height environmental frame, one extreme flower interference, and one
top-down grass frame. The constants are open field/sky, warm hard sun or its backlit continuation,
white translucent/reflective surfaces, blue and green fields, direct wind interaction, and a calm
unsmiling face.

Exposure has two observed states: deep blue sky when the subject is exposed from the front/side,
and pale-to-white sky when the camera faces the low sun. Both are members of the same gamma. The
fixed optic is otherwise clean: crisp hard-sun edges, no repeated swirl, chromatic fringe,
vignette, global diffusion, or repeated flare. The single pink disc in `frame-01.jpg` is conditional
sun contact and is excluded from the fixed runtime signature; no frame should receive an added
flare merely to look “editorial.”

Source-model anatomy, face, skin, hair and body are not style. Source garment cut, colour, lingerie
construction, jacket details, pearls, and bouquet arrangement are not style. Transfer only camera
consequences, pose chains, environment, light, cloth response, expression muscles, colour system,
and foreground geometry.

## TRANSFERABLE EXPRESSION AND GARMENT BEHAVIOUR

Expression is calm and sun-held: no smile, cheekbones at rest, lids neutral-to-low or narrowed by
sun, brows at rest, mouth closed or softly parted, gaze directed down toward/past camera or
withheld by hair/light/flowers. Provenance: frames 02, 05, 06, 08, 09.

Wind lifts light layers laterally away from the body in broad translucent sheets, creating visible
overlaps in backlight. Opaque structured layers keep broad folds and move only at loose sleeves or
hems. Apply the force/light response to the approved item according to its real weight: a heavy
item lifts only at a free edge and must not become sheer; a genuinely translucent item may glow and
show overlapping layers. Do not copy the source garment's cut or colour. Provenance: frames 01,
02, 05, 08, 09.
