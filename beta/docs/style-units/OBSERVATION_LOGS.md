# Observation logs — five photoshoot units

Read directly off the supplied frames. Values name what they were read from. Anything a photograph
cannot show is marked UNKNOWN rather than guessed. Focal lengths are never stated; their
consequences are.

The reference models are NOT part of any unit. Where this log describes skin, it describes how skin
behaves under that shoot's light, never whose skin it was.

---

## UNIT 1 — `shoot.skylight_haze`
Working name: backlit skylight haze, floral organza

**CAMERA POSITION** — Extremely low, below hip height, aimed steeply up. Read from: the ceiling
structure receding to a vanishing point above the head, the jaw and chin underside being visible,
and the nostril undersides reading. **Roll: tilted, roughly 5–8° anticlockwise** — read from the
skylight mullions running diagonally where the structure is orthogonal.

**PERSPECTIVE CHARACTER** — Strong wide rendering. Verticals converge hard upward. The raised near
hand (upper left) is enlarged well beyond head scale; the head reads small relative to the torso.
Background is expanded, not compressed.

**DEPTH OF FIELD** — Very shallow. Plane of focus on the eyes and upper chest. Falloff is fast:
the raised hand one arm-length nearer is already dissolved.

**FOREGROUND OCCLUSION — the signature of this unit.** A large creamy-warm mass, heavily out of
focus, occupies the entire left third and lower-left corner, roughly 30–35% of the frame. It reads
as fabric or foliage lit from behind. Second occlusion: the subject's own raised forearm crosses the
upper-left. Without these the frame is an ordinary low-angle portrait.

**OPTICAL ARTEFACTS** — Heavy veiling flare washing the upper centre, source behind the head.
Blown highlight ring around the head and shoulder. Halation on individual hair strands. Warm bloom
bleeding into the garment's pale areas. Grain: fine, low, mostly visible in the haze.

**LIGHT** — Single broad source behind and above, through a skylight; the sky reads blown to near
white. Rim on hair, cheek edge and shoulder. Face carried by soft bounce, so facial contrast is
LOW while frame contrast is high. Light wraps rather than cuts.

**HOW SKIN TAKES THIS LIGHT** — Specular highlights are broad and soft, sitting on the forehead,
nose bridge and cheekbone tops. Warm subsurface glow through the ear and along the jaw where the
backlight passes. The shadow terminator on the face is very soft, almost absent. Halation creates a
warm fringe where skin meets the blown background.

**ENVIRONMENT** — Interior under a glass roof: pale beams and mullions, atrium or greenhouse
character. Almost no material detail survives the haze. Depth planes: blown sky, structure,
subject, occluding foreground. UNKNOWN: whether the room has walls in shot.

**COLOUR** — warm cream `#EFE0CE`, peach `#E8B79A`, olive-sage `#6F7355`, deep foliage `#3B4630`,
dusty rose `#C98A78`, blown pale cyan-white `#EAF0F2`. Blacks lifted; highlights warm and rolled
off, not clipped hard. Split-tone: warm highlights, cool-neutral shadows.

**BLOCKING & POSE** — Body square to lens. Both arms raised overhead, elbows out. Chin dropped,
eyes down toward the lens. Weight UNKNOWN — the frame cuts at mid-thigh.

**FRAME** — Portrait, roughly 3:4. Subject fills most of the height, cropped mid-thigh. Small
headroom. Subject centred slightly right of centre.

**MOOD** — Dissolving, sunstruck, reaching. The camera is beneath the subject and the air is doing
half the work.

## UNIT 2 — `shoot.terracotta_hardlight`
Working name: terracotta wall, hard sun, white linen in motion

**CAMERA POSITION** — Chest to shoulder height, very slight upward tilt. **Roll: level** — read
from the wall's shadow edge being straight and the subject's vertical axis being upright.

**PERSPECTIVE CHARACTER** — Normal to slightly long. No vertical convergence. Background is a flat
plane and reads flat, so no compression cue is available: UNKNOWN beyond "not wide".

**DEPTH OF FIELD** — Moderate. Subject sharp; the wall behind is softly rendered yet its shadow
edge stays legible, so the wall is close to the subject or the aperture is mid.

**FOREGROUND OCCLUSION** — None. Everything happens in the subject plane.

**OPTICAL ARTEFACTS** — No flare. Highlights on the white cloth roll off smoothly with slight
halation at the brightest folds. Grain: fine, low. No visible vignette.

**LIGHT — the signature of this unit.** One hard source, high and camera-left, direct-sun quality.
It throws a **sharp diagonal shadow across the terracotta wall**, running from upper-left down to
mid-right, which is the frame's main graphic element. Shadow edges are crisp, so the source is small
and undiffused. Contrast is high; shadow detail retained, not crushed.

**HOW SKIN TAKES THIS LIGHT** — Tight, small specular highlights. A hard-edged shadow terminator
across the cheek and neck. The lit side is warm from the wall's terracotta bounce; the shadow side
picks up the same warmth, so the whole face sits in one warm family rather than warm/cool split.

**ENVIRONMENT** — A single seamless terracotta wall, no props, no floor visible. The only feature
is the cast shadow of something off-frame with a straight edge. UNKNOWN: interior or exterior.

**COLOUR** — terracotta `#B5543A`, deep shadowed brown `#5A2A20`, off-white linen `#F2EDE4`, warm
skin `#C89275`, rust trouser `#B8552F`. Four to five values only. Deep warm shadows; highlights not
clipped. Monochromatic-warm grade, near-analogous.

**BLOCKING & POSE** — Back three-quarters to the lens, head turned back over the shoulder, gaze
straight to camera. Arms lifted inside the shirt so the fabric opens as a wing. Hair thrown by the
same motion. A caught moment, frozen with slight softness at the fabric edges.

**FRAME** — Portrait, roughly 3:4. Subject in the lower centre; the upper-left third is given to
the shadow graphic. Generous negative space above.

**MOOD** — Hot, turning away and looking back. Sculptural, one wall, one sun.

## UNIT 3 — `shoot.window_gobo_warm`
Working name: warm wall, window gobo, black ruched tulle

**CAMERA POSITION** — Chest height, level, straight on. **Roll: level.**

**PERSPECTIVE CHARACTER** — Slightly long. Verticals parallel, no enlargement of near limbs, the
wall reads flat and mildly compressed.

**DEPTH OF FIELD** — Shallow to moderate. Face and garment texture sharp; the wall softens but the
gobo edges stay readable.

**FOREGROUND OCCLUSION** — None.

**OPTICAL ARTEFACTS** — No flare. Gentle bloom in the brightest wall patches. Grain fine and low.
Slight warm halation where the lit wall meets the dark garment.

**LIGHT — the signature of this unit.** Hard low sun through a window, throwing a **large
soft-edged rectangular gobo on the wall** — an upright bar with a crossing horizontal, reading as a
window frame. Direction from camera-right and low, raking almost along the wall. The gobo is the
composition; the subject stands against its bright field.

**HOW SKIN TAKES THIS LIGHT** — Medium-tight speculars on cheekbone, nose bridge and collarbone.
Shadow terminator crisp but not hard. Strong warm bounce off the wall fills the shadow side, so
facial contrast is moderate despite a hard source. Fine facial hair and stray strands catch the rake
and glow.

**ENVIRONMENT** — One warm cream-greige wall. No props, no floor visible, no furniture. The window
shadow is the only feature. UNKNOWN: room size and what is behind the camera.

**COLOUR** — warm cream `#E4D3BE`, greige mid `#C9B49C`, deep warm shadow `#6B4E3C`, black garment
`#1A1512`, warm skin `#D2A183`. Warm neutral family. Shadows warm and open; highlights soft.
Contrast curve: gentle S, no crushed blacks.

**BLOCKING & POSE** — Three-quarters to the lens. One hand on the hip, elbow out; the other arm
hangs. Head slightly tilted, chin level, direct gaze. Loose hair with strands across the face.
Weight on the hip-hand side.

**FRAME** — Portrait, roughly 3:4, cropped mid-thigh. Subject centred, generous space above the
head where the gobo runs.

**MOOD** — Composed, warm, still. Late sun and a wall doing the styling.

## UNIT 4 — `shoot.grey_studio_stride`
Working name: grey studio, long-lens stride, dried reeds

**CAMERA POSITION** — Waist to hip height, level, perpendicular to the walk. **Roll: level.**

**PERSPECTIVE CHARACTER** — Long lens. Verticals strictly parallel, no limb enlargement, the
backdrop reads as an even flat field. The body's proportions are undistorted end to end.

**DEPTH OF FIELD** — Deep enough that the reeds and the floor stay readable while remaining
subordinate; the backdrop gradient is smooth and featureless.

**FOREGROUND OCCLUSION** — None, but the dried reeds along the base form a low occluding band the
feet pass behind.

**OPTICAL ARTEFACTS** — No flare, no visible halation, no vignette. Grain very fine. This is the
cleanest of the five optically.

**LIGHT** — Broad soft studio source, frontal and slightly camera-left, low contrast. A soft short
shadow under the body on the dark floor. The backdrop carries a smooth vertical gradient, brighter
behind the subject, so it is separately lit.

**HOW SKIN TAKES THIS LIGHT** — Broad soft speculars, no hotspots. Shadow terminator very soft.
Even tone from lit to shadow side; deep skin holds its value without going flat because the fill is
directional rather than ambient.

**ENVIRONMENT** — Seamless mid-grey studio backdrop, smooth gradient, no seam visible. A strip of
dried reeds or grass runs along the base. Matte dark floor, almost black, mildly reflective.

**COLOUR** — mid grey `#8A8C8E`, cool grey shadow `#5F6266`, ivory `#F0E8D8`, khaki-taupe print
`#A79877`, straw `#B79A6A`, near-black floor `#1C1B1A`, warm deep skin `#7A4E33`. Neutral base with
a warm accent family. Neutral grade, no split-tone, gentle contrast.

**BLOCKING & POSE** — Full profile to the lens, mid-stride, walking across frame. Long straight
hair swept horizontally by the motion. Arms swinging naturally, one hand holding a small flat object.
Full length, footwear entirely in frame. Weight on the leading foot.

**FRAME** — Portrait, roughly 4:5. Full length with generous headroom and floor. Subject centred,
moving into the space ahead of them.

**MOOD** — Clean, commercial, in motion. The one unit here that reads as a lookbook rather than art.

## UNIT 5 — `shoot.sky_dune_surreal`
Working name: cloud sky, sand ridge, deadpan frontal

**CAMERA POSITION** — Chest height, level, dead frontal. **Roll: level.**

**PERSPECTIVE CHARACTER** — Normal. No convergence, no limb enlargement. The horizon sits low
behind the subject, around knee height, which flattens the ground into a band.

**DEPTH OF FIELD** — Deep. Clouds and sand grain both hold detail.

**FOREGROUND OCCLUSION** — None. Nothing between lens and subject.

**OPTICAL ARTEFACTS** — No flare, no halation, no vignette. Grain very fine. Clean and graphic.

**LIGHT** — Soft even daylight, mildly diffused, slightly above and frontal. The shadow under the
feet is short and soft, so the sun is high but veiled. Low contrast throughout; no hard edge
anywhere.

**HOW SKIN TAKES THIS LIGHT** — Very soft broad speculars on forehead and cheekbones. Almost no
visible terminator. Deep skin retains full value and saturation because the light is even rather
than hot.

**ENVIRONMENT — staged, and that is the point.** Pale blue sky with discrete cumulus clouds, and a
sand ridge whose crest reads as a clean curve. Nothing else in frame: no horizon furniture, no
plants, no structures. The cleanliness suggests a built or composited set rather than a location.
UNKNOWN: whether the sky is real, painted or composited. Record the look, not the method.

**COLOUR** — sky blue `#A8B6C8`, cloud white `#F4F5F5`, warm sand `#C4A385`, pale lilac `#D9C3D6`,
peach `#E7C4A8`, cream `#EFE9DC`, deep skin `#4A2E22`. Pastel, desaturated, cool sky against warm
ground. Contrast low, blacks lifted, no split-tone.

**BLOCKING & POSE** — Dead frontal, standing still, feet planted apart, arms down. One hand carries
an object at thigh height. Deadpan, gaze to lens. Weight even on both feet. Full length, footwear
entirely in frame.

**FRAME** — Portrait, roughly 4:5. Subject centred, full length, generous headroom, horizon low.

**MOOD** — Deadpan surreal. A studio portrait that has been put outdoors on purpose.

# EXPRESSION & GAZE — read per unit

Recorded as muscle positions. Adjectives appear only after the position they summarise.

## Unit 1 — `shoot.skylight_haze`
**Gaze format** — into the lens, but from beneath. The chin is dropped and the eyes travel up-out to
meet the camera, so the look arrives over the lower lid.
**Eyelids** — upper lids carried LOW, covering roughly the top third of the iris; heavy-lidded and
symmetric. No sclera visible above the iris; a thin band below. Lower lids soft, no tension.
**Brows** — at rest, level, no inner-brow pull, no arch lift. The brow does none of the work.
**Cheekbones** — AT REST. The midface is flat, the eye stays open in its own shape, no lower-lid
crease. Nothing here is a smile.
**Mouth** — lips parted, roughly 2–3 mm, jaw not dropped — the lips are simply apart. Corners level
to a hair below level. No tension in the philtrum.
**Jaw and neck** — jaw relaxed, no masseter. Chin dropped toward the chest, so the neck shortens at
the front and the underside of the jaw reads to the low camera.
**Head orientation** — pan neutral, tilt down, roll following the frame's tilt.
**Signature** — the low lid plus the raised arms. The face is doing almost nothing while the body
does everything. Languid, absent, sun-drugged.

## Unit 2 — `shoot.terracotta_hardlight`
**Gaze format** — into the lens across the shoulder. The body has turned away and the eyes have
stayed, so the look is a hold, not a pose.
**Eyelids** — upper lids slightly lowered, covering the top sliver of the iris; more open than
unit 1. Full iris visible, no sclera above. Lower lids untensed.
**Brows** — at rest and level. No inner pull. Very slight asymmetry from the head turn, not from
expression.
**Cheekbones** — AT REST. No lift, no crease.
**Mouth** — closed, lips full and soft, corners level. No compression, no pursing.
**Jaw and neck** — jaw relaxed. Chin level to a touch raised. The turn engages the near
sternocleidomastoid, which reads as a line down the neck — part of the frame's geometry.
**Head orientation** — pan strongly toward the lens against a body facing away, tilt neutral, roll
neutral. The pan-versus-body difference is most of the attitude.
**Signature** — cool, appraising, unhurried. The motion is in the hair and cloth; the face is still.

## Unit 3 — `shoot.window_gobo_warm`
**Gaze format** — straight into the lens, level. The most direct of the five.
**Eyelids** — upper lids slightly lowered, the editorial half-lid; top of the iris just covered.
Symmetric. Lower lid relaxed. No white above or below.
**Brows** — at rest, level, no arch lift, no inner pull.
**Cheekbones** — AT REST, and this is the whole point of the face: a direct gaze with an
unengaged midface reads as self-possession rather than warmth.
**Mouth** — closed, lips full, corners level to a fraction below. Soft, no tension.
**Jaw and neck** — jaw relaxed, chin level, neck long and unshortened.
**Head orientation** — pan slight toward the lens side, small tilt, roll neutral. Body
three-quarters while the head comes fully round.
**Signature** — zero performance. Nothing is being offered to the camera and nothing is withheld.
The stillest face of the five.

## Unit 4 — `shoot.grey_studio_stride`
**Gaze format** — AWAY, and deliberately unavailable. The eyes are down and forward along the walk;
the lens gets a profile and no contact.
**Eyelids** — upper lids lowered over a downward gaze, so most of the iris is hidden; lashes read
as a dark line. Lower lids relaxed.
**Brows** — at rest, no pull.
**Cheekbones** — AT REST.
**Mouth** — closed, corners level, lips soft and unremarkable. Nothing is expressed.
**Jaw and neck** — jaw relaxed, chin level, neck long and carried forward by the stride.
**Head orientation** — full profile to the lens, tilt slightly down, roll neutral. The head follows
the walk rather than the camera.
**Signature** — unaware, inward, in motion. This is the only unit whose face is not addressing the
camera at all, and that absence is the format.

## Unit 5 — `shoot.sky_dune_surreal`
**Gaze format** — WITHHELD. The eyes are behind opaque white-framed dark lenses. Direction reads as
straight to the lens from the head's orientation, but the eyes themselves are unavailable. This is a
deliberate choice, not missing data.
**Eyelids** — UNKNOWN, occluded by the lenses. Do not infer.
**Brows** — UNKNOWN, above the frame line of the lenses and under the knitted cap.
**Cheekbones** — AT REST. Readable below the lenses: the midface is flat, no lift.
**Mouth** — closed, corners level, absolutely neutral. No compression, no smile, no tension.
Deadpan in the strict sense.
**Jaw and neck** — jaw relaxed, no masseter, chin level. Neck covered by the high collar.
**Head orientation** — pan, tilt and roll all neutral. Dead frontal, squared to the lens.
**Signature** — affectless and unreadable on purpose. The sunglasses ARE the expression decision:
the unit's mood comes from refusing the viewer an eye to read.

# GARMENT BEHAVIOUR — read per unit

What the cloth DOES. No design, no print, no silhouette — those belong to whoever made the garment.

## Unit 1 — `shoot.skylight_haze`
**Motion state** — settling after the arms went up. Not driven by wind; driven by the subject's own
movement, and caught late in it.
**Air interaction** — sleeves balloon outward from the arm and hold air; the body of the garment
stays close. Departure from the body is greatest at the sleeve, roughly a hand's width.
**Drape and weight class** — very light. Fold radius is tight and numerous, the signature of a thin
fabric with no body of its own.
**Transparency under this light** — the shoot is backlit, so light passes through and the cloth
GLOWS from behind, revealing the layer and the arm beneath. This is a property of the pair: the same
cloth lit from the front in unit 4's studio would read solid.
**Edge quality** — soft, slightly dissolved where motion and haze meet the hem.
**Surface response** — low sheen, the weave takes a diffuse glow rather than a specular hit.
**How it transfers** — a heavy knit hoodie in this unit does not balloon. It lifts at the hem, holds
one broad slow fold, and its edge glows where the backlight rakes past it. Anything thin the
approved look contains should be allowed to fill with air at the sleeve and go translucent against
the blown background.

## Unit 2 — `shoot.terracotta_hardlight`
**Motion state** — DRIVEN, at its peak. The subject's turn threw the cloth and the frame catches it
fully open.
**Air interaction** — the garment leaves the body dramatically, opening as a wing well past the
shoulder line; the trailing edge streams. Direction is away from the turn and across the light.
**Drape and weight class** — light to mid, structured enough to hold a curved plane instead of
collapsing. Fold radius broad and few — a fabric with some body.
**Transparency under this light** — the hard sun is roughly side-on, so the cloth reads mostly
opaque with bright edge translucency only where a single layer catches the sun directly.
**Edge quality** — crisp, with a faint motion softening at the fastest-moving edge.
**Surface response** — matte with a slight sheen on the tautest planes.
**How it transfers** — in this unit the approved item must be caught in the same driven moment: a
heavy garment lifts and holds one large curved plane away from the body rather than fluttering, and
the hard sun draws a bright hard-edged line along its uppermost fold.

## Unit 3 — `shoot.window_gobo_warm`
**Motion state** — STILL. Fully settled, no air in it.
**Air interaction** — none. The cloth hangs and gathers where the body puts it.
**Drape and weight class** — light, with dense small-scale gathering that stays where it is set.
Fold radius very tight and packed.
**Transparency under this light** — sheer, and the raking light makes it read as a dark veil over
skin: the arm is visible through the sleeve where a single layer sits, opaque where layers stack.
**Edge quality** — soft, unstressed, no motion.
**Surface response** — matte; the rake picks out the texture of the gathering rather than making a
highlight.
**How it transfers** — the approved item hangs at rest here, and the shoot's job is to let the raking
light describe its surface. A heavy garment shows its weave and its seams under the rake; a light one
lets the raking light through where it is single-layered. Nothing moves.

## Unit 4 — `shoot.grey_studio_stride`
**Motion state** — CAUGHT MID-MOTION, driven by the walk, controlled rather than dramatic.
**Air interaction** — trailing, not ballooning. Fabric flows behind the direction of travel and the
sleeve holds a rounded volume of its own. Departure from the body is moderate and always rearward.
**Drape and weight class** — mid-weight and fluid, falling in long slow folds that follow the leg.
Fold radius broad. Sleeve volume is structural, held by the cut rather than by air.
**Transparency under this light** — opaque throughout. The soft frontal studio light gives no
backlight, so nothing goes translucent anywhere.
**Edge quality** — crisp hems, clean, no fraying or dissolving. The cleanest cloth of the five.
**Surface response** — soft sheen on the ivory; the print reads matte.
**How it transfers** — the approved item moves with the stride and trails behind it, holding long
slow folds. No garment in this unit is ever translucent and no edge is ever soft, because the light
is frontal and the shutter is fast.

## Unit 5 — `shoot.sky_dune_surreal`
**Motion state** — STILL, deliberately inert. Nothing moves at all.
**Air interaction** — none. The garment sits on the body as volume, held by its own construction.
**Drape and weight class** — heavy and plush. Fold radius very broad and soft; the pile absorbs the
fold rather than creasing.
**Transparency under this light** — fully opaque. The diffuse daylight gives no translucency
anywhere.
**Edge quality** — soft and rounded from the pile, but not moving; hems sit where they fall.
**Surface response** — matte and light-absorbing, no specular at all. The diffuse light makes the
surface read as texture rather than sheen.
**How it transfers** — the approved item is presented, not performed: it hangs at full rest, holds
broad soft volume, and gives no highlight. A light garment in this unit must be styled to hang dead
rather than allowed to move, or the frame stops belonging to the unit.

## Reconciliation across the five

These are **five separate units**, not variants of one. Evidence: camera height spans below-hip to
hip to chest; roll is tilted in unit 1 and level in the other four; light spans a single hard sun
(2, 3) to broad soft studio (4) to veiled daylight (5) to backlit haze (1); optical signature spans
heavy flare and foreground occlusion (1) to optically clean (4, 5). Grouping any two of them would
produce a style nothing can hit.

Shared house traits worth noting so the sheets do not contradict each other: all five are portrait
orientation, all five put a single subject alone in frame, none contains readable text or a visible
brand mark, none has a second person.

## Rights and identity

The supplied frames are extraction-only. No reference model's face, body or hair is carried into any
sheet; the skin sections describe how skin behaves under each shoot's light, which is photography,
not identity. Three of the five frames are recognisable garment styling rather than branded product;
no logo or readable text is carried over.
