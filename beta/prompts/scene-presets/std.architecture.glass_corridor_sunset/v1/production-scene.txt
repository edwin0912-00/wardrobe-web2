LOOK_MASTER is the only authority for identity, face, body, hair, every approved clothing item, footwear, bag, accessory, color, material, construction, graphic, logo and readable product text. Subject bounding box occupies 70–80% of image height, with at least 8% clear space above hair and 2% clean floor space below footwear.

6. FINISH
Premium contemporary fashion editorial, precise natural material texture, controlled highlights, clean shadows, and restrained magazine grade. No HDR, bloom, skin smoothing, cutout edge, halo, floating feet, or light mismatches.

7. FAIL CONDITIONS
