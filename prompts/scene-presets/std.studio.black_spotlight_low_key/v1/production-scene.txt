1. TASK
Create one photorealistic high-fashion campaign still by placing the exact person and complete approved look from LOOK_MASTER into the original environment defined by ENVIRONMENT_ANCHOR and LIGHTING_COMPOSITION_ANCHOR.

2. AUTHORITY
LOOK_MASTER is the only authority for identity, face, body, hair, every approved clothing item, footwear, bag, accessory, color, material, construction, graphic, logo and readable product text. Preserve all of them exactly. The environment and lighting references have zero authority over the person or look.

3. SCENE
Invented pitch-black seamless studio environment. Keep believable depth. Do not reproduce a recognizable real location or landmark. No other people, vehicles, bicycles, storefront branding, signs, café furniture or new props.

4. LIGHT
Extreme low-key lighting with a single hard spotlight from above, dust visible in the beam, deep black shadows. Preserve natural skin tone and exact item colors. Keep flare away from the face, logos, text and product construction. Ensure a grounded contact shadow.

5. CAMERA AND FRAMING
Final delivery is vertical 4:5. 50mm equivalent, straight verticals, no wide-angle distortion. Full body with complete head, complete bag/carried item, and both shoes. Subject bounding box occupies 74–78% of image height, with at least 8% clear space above hair and 2% clean floor space below footwear.

6. FINISH
Premium contemporary fashion editorial, precise natural material texture, controlled highlights, clean shadows, and restrained magazine grade. No HDR, bloom, skin smoothing, cutout edge, halo, floating feet, or light mismatches.

7. FAIL CONDITIONS
Any identity drift; removed, changed or invented item; altered logo or text; changed color or construction; anatomy defect; missing fingers; cropped headwear or shoes; incorrect scale; duplicated accessory; random prop; readable environment text; copied landmark; ungrounded shadow.
