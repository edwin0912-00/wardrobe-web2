1. TASK
Create one photorealistic high-fashion campaign still by placing the exact person and complete approved look from LOOK_MASTER into the original environment defined by ENVIRONMENT_ANCHOR and LIGHTING_COMPOSITION_ANCHOR.

2. AUTHORITY
LOOK_MASTER is the only authority for identity, face, body, hair, every approved clothing item, footwear, bag, accessory, color, material, construction, graphic, logo and readable product text. Preserve all of them exactly. The environment and lighting define the ambient light wrap, rim light, bounce light, and color grade that MUST be actively applied onto the subject so the person is seamlessly integrated into the scene without a cutout or pasted-on effect.

3. SCENE
Invented minimalist studio with a terracotta textured concrete wall. Keep believable depth. Do not reproduce a recognizable real location or landmark. No other people, vehicles, bicycles, storefront branding, signs, café furniture or new props.

4. LIGHT AND RELIGHTING INTEGRATION
Low-angle raking side light across a textured terracotta wall. ACTIVELY APPLY AND WRAP THIS LIGHTING ONTO THE SUBJECT:
- Emphasize fabric textures, leather grain, and garment seams with strong directional side light.
- Reflect warm terracotta bounce light onto the shadowed side of the subject.
- Ground the subject with a crisp side contact shadow extending across the floor.
- Absolute NO cutout, sticker, or pasted-on look. Harmonize color temperature and lighting direction while strictly preserving identity and garment construction.
5. CAMERA AND FRAMING
Final delivery is vertical 4:5. 50mm equivalent, straight verticals, no wide-angle distortion. Full body with complete head, complete bag/carried item, and both shoes. Subject bounding box occupies 74–78% of image height, with at least 8% clear space above hair and 2% clean floor space below footwear.

6. FINISH
Premium contemporary fashion editorial, precise natural material texture, controlled highlights, clean shadows, and restrained magazine grade. No HDR, bloom, skin smoothing, cutout edge, halo, floating feet, or light mismatches.

7. FAIL CONDITIONS
Any identity drift; removed, changed or invented item; altered logo or text; changed color or construction; anatomy defect; missing fingers; cropped headwear or shoes; incorrect scale; duplicated accessory; random prop; readable environment text; copied landmark; ungrounded shadow.
