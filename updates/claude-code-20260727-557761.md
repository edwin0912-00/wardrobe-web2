## 1. Style units

Two units read from five supplied male frames, committed with their source
frames, four generated sheets each, a locally rendered palette strip, and a
declaration whose hex values are measured from source pixels.

The declaration gains a `compatibility` field. The five earlier units were all
read from female editorial frames, and their blocking sheets specify a raised
hip and a hand on the hip crest, which is what made every male test frame read
wrong. A unit must declare the presentation it was read from; the catalogue
filters on that rather than inferring anything from a user photo.

The dune unit gains a v2 environment sheet stating a sun contract — one hard
low sun, cast shadow leaving the soles, ripple micro-shadow, warm up-bounce,
foot displacement — because the unit as declared produced a person cut out
onto a backdrop. The reshoot reads as one photograph. The unit itself is not
yet rebuilt around the new sheet.

Two measured findings:

- Wardrobe drift is a reference-weight problem, not a wording problem. Four
  style sheets against one approved look silently replaced the approved
  trousers and footwear. Naming each approved garment explicitly held the
  wardrobe in every later frame. A per-garment lock is the durable fix.
- Brand graphics on a garment are not reproduced identically frame to frame;
  folds eat part of the print. Prompt text does not hold it.
